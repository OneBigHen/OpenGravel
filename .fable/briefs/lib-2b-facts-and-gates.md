# L2b: Facts exactly as the source said them, and gates that stop a bad pack

**Rider sentence:** "What the page says about a ride is exactly what the source said."
**Worker:** DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (mechanical tables and checks). **Size:** M. **Needs:** L1a merged.
Rules: `.fable/briefs/lib-0-rules.md`. Plan sections 5.1 (stages 4, 6, 8), 5.2 and 5.4.

## Why
The records carry 272 different surface phrasings, 326 season phrasings and free-text permits. 5,232 say
difficulty unknown, 4,703 season unknown, 761 surface unknown. About 1,490 hide a rider rating inside a text
note. 1,422 have no coordinates. The page must repeat what a source said, label it as the source's, and say
"not stated" for the rest. Nothing here may guess.

## Build
1. `src/application/route-library/facts.ts`: for surface, legal status, difficulty, permits and season, return
   `{ value | null, quote, source }`. Tables live in the file. Surface: `paved`, `unpaved`, `mixed`, else `null`.
   Legal: `public-roads`, `designated-ohv`, `mixed`, else `null`. Difficulty: the schema's five words, anything
   else `null`. Season and permits are not normalised: the quote is carried, and the values "unknown", "none
   stated" and empty become `null`.
2. Rider wording helpers (pure, tested): surface `paved` gives "Paved, the source says"; `null` gives "Surface
   unknown"; `designated-ohv` gives "Open to motorcycles, the source says"; a permit quote gives "Permit: <quote>
   (the source says)"; `null` season gives nothing on the card and "Season not stated" on the page.
3. `source_rating`: read the structured field when the record has it; otherwise parse only the exact pattern
   "rider rating N/5 (M review(s))" from `terms_provenance` or `scraper.notes`. No match means no rating.
4. Track flags per plan stage 4 (`rough`, `old`, `mismatch`) with their thresholds as named constants.
5. `location`: copy the record's `location` when present (plan 5.4, item 1) into the index `p` with `pp` set
   from `precision`. Absent stays `null`. Do not geocode.
6. Gates in the builder (plan 5.2), each a named check with its own line in `pack-report.md` and a non-zero exit.
   `--allow-drop` is the only override and it is printed in the report.
7. `scripts/route-library/phrases.snapshot.json`: every distinct phrase seen for each fact with its count and
   mapped value, regenerated by the builder, so a reviewer can read the whole mapping in one file.
