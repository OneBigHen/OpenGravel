# Code of conduct

OpenGravel follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and sharing someone's private information (including where they live or ride) are not welcome.

Report problems privately through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/OneBigHen/OpenGravel/security/advisories/new), marked "conduct". Reports are handled confidentially.
